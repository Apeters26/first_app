What is market breadth?

An index like the S&P 500 is weighted by company size, so a handful of giants can push it up even while most stocks fall. Breadth measures how many stocks are taking part. Healthy rallies usually have broad participation. When the index climbs while breadth shrinks (a divergence), the move is resting on fewer shoulders and tends to be more fragile.

  • Healthy: most stocks are participating.
  • Mixed: participation is middling or the signals disagree.
  • Weak: most stocks are lagging, even if the index looks fine.

These are context, not trade signals. Breadth can stay weak or strong for a long time.
